Something has shifted in the last twelve months. AI has gone from "interesting tech demo" to the thing rewiring how work works, how businesses get built, and how one person can now do what teams used to do.

The people who'll thrive aren't the ones with the safest jobs or the most tools. They're the ones who know where they stand with AI right now, where it belongs in their work, and where it doesn't.

That's what this Audit is for.

Six questions · Honest answers · No wrong ones

At the end, you'll get your archetype (one of four cats, naturally), a personalised read, and one specific shift worth making next. Plus a downloadable version you can keep. No email required. No upsell to do the work. Just clarity.

The questions about AI don't disappear when you become competent. They get more interesting. Let's see where yours are.
